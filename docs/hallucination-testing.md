# Hallucination testing

The lab distinguishes unsupported factual claims, invented policies, dates or contacts, contradictions, fabricated source IDs and failure to refuse when evidence is absent. Fluent wording receives no special credit.

Adversarial cases cover missing evidence, partial evidence, current versus superseded policies, similar terms, distractors, prompt injection and citation injection. The system prompt marks documents as untrusted evidence. Deterministic checks identify forbidden claims and source-ID failures; semantic claim mapping can be added with a calibrated judge.

A low aggregate hallucination rate must not hide a critical case. Reports therefore retain unsupported claims and reason codes at case level, and the quality gate can block on any critical policy category.
