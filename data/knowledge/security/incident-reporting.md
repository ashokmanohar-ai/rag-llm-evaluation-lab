# Information Security Incident Reporting

Suspected information security incidents must be reported immediately through the Security Hotline form or by calling the service desk. Do not investigate, delete evidence or contact a suspected attacker. If personal data may be involved, mark the report as a potential privacy incident so the Privacy team is notified.
