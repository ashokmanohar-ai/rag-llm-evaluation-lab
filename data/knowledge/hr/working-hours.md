# Working Hours and Flexitime

Standard full-time working hours are 37.5 hours per week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. local time on working days. Flexitime balances must remain between minus five and plus ten hours unless a manager approves an exception.
