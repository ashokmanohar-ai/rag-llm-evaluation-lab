# Employee Benefits Guide

Permanent employees can join the pension plan after completing 30 days of employment. Acme contributes six percent of pensionable salary when an employee contributes at least four percent. Private medical insurance begins on the first day of the month after probation is passed. Benefit selections are managed in PeopleHub.
