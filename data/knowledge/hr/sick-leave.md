# Sickness Absence Policy

Employees must notify their line manager before 9:30 a.m. on the first day of sickness absence. Self-certification is accepted for the first seven calendar days. An eligible medical certificate is required from the eighth calendar day. Absence must also be recorded in PeopleHub when the employee is able to access it.
