# Annual Leave Policy

Full-time UK employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per holiday year, excluding public holidays. Employees may carry over a maximum of five unused days with manager approval. Carried leave must be used by 31 March of the following year. Requests for five or more consecutive days should be submitted at least four weeks in advance through PeopleHub.
