# Parental Leave Overview

Employees should notify HR of an expected birth or adoption at least 15 weeks before the expected week when practicable. Statutory eligibility and pay are confirmed individually by HR. Requests and supporting documents are submitted through the confidential Family Leave workflow in PeopleHub.
