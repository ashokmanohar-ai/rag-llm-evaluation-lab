# Hybrid and Remote Working Policy

Eligible UK employees may work remotely for up to three days per week when agreed with their manager. Teams must maintain two shared collaboration days each week. Working temporarily outside the United Kingdom requires HR and Information Security approval before travel. Home-working arrangements are reviewed every six months.
