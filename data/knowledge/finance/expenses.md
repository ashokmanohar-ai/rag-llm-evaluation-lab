# Business Expense Policy

Expense claims must be submitted in ExpenseCloud within 30 calendar days of the transaction. A receipt is required for each item costing £25 or more. The daily meal limit for travel within the UK is £45 including taxes and service charges. The employee's manager approves the claim before Finance review.
