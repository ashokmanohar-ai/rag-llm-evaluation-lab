# Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA is required for remote access and cloud applications. Employees must reject unexpected approval prompts and report them to Security. A replacement phone is enrolled through the MFA Recovery workflow after identity verification. MFA codes must never be shared, including with service desk staff.
