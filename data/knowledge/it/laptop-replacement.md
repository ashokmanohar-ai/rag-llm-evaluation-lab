# Laptop Repair and Replacement

Report a lost or stolen laptop immediately to the service desk and Information Security. A faulty laptop is assessed remotely before replacement. Standard lifecycle replacement occurs after four years. Back up approved work files to OneDrive because local files may not be recoverable during repair.
