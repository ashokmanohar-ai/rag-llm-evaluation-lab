# Support Priority Definitions

Priority 1 means a critical service is unavailable to most users with no workaround. Priority 2 means major degradation affecting multiple users. Priority 3 covers a limited impact or an available workaround. Priority 4 covers information requests and minor issues. Users should describe impact and urgency; Support assigns the final priority.
