# Service Desk Hours and Channels

The UK service desk operates from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Requests can be logged in SupportHub at any time. The on-call team handles Priority 1 incidents outside normal hours; routine requests wait until the next service window.
